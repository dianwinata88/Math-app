📖 MathQuest User Guide

A math adventure for kids ages 3–12 — PreK through Grade 5. Short playful levels, a daily plan picked just for your child, and a treasure chest of stars, coins, and stickers.

1. Getting started

The first launch walks through three quick steps — no accounts, no email, everything stays on the device.

StepWhat happens
1 · NameType the name MathQuest should call your child (up to 24 characters).
2 · BuddyPick an adventure buddy from 8 animal avatars — 🦊 🐼 🦁 🐸 🐵 🐨 🐯 🐰.
3 · GradeChoose a starting grade: PreK, K, or Grade 1–5. Each card shows the age range. You can change it any time in the Parent Area.

Tap Let's go! 🚀 and the Adventure Map opens.

2. The home screen

Everything starts here:

  • Greeting & stats — your child's buddy, name, grade, plus running totals: 🪙 coins, 🔥 day streak, and ⭐ stars for the current grade.
  • Daily Practice card — one tap starts a set of 10 questions picked just for your child (see below).
  • Adventure Map 🗺️ — every unit in the grade as a colorful path. Each unit has its own theme, emoji, and numbered level stops.
  • Rewards 🎁 / Profile 👤 — buttons at the bottom open the treasure chest and the Parent Area.

3. Playing a level

  • Levels inside a unit unlock one by one: the first is always open, and each next stop opens once the level before it earns at least 1 star. Locked stops show 🔒.
  • A level is a short session of questions (usually 4–8). The top bar shows progress and a question counter; ✕ exits early.
  • After each answer, MathQuest shows instant feedback — ✅ Correct! or the right answer with the picture, so mistakes become mini-lessons.
  • Finish the session to see the results screen: a cheer, the score, earned stars, and +XP. Tap Collect rewards 🎁 to bank them.

4. Stars, coins & XP

RewardHow it's earned
⭐ StarsBased on the share of correct answers in a level: ≥50% → 1 star, ≥70% → 2 stars, ≥90% → 3 stars. Your best result is kept — replaying can only raise it. A unit is mastered when every level has 3 stars.
🪙 Coinsstars × 5 + correct answers per session — a 3-star round pays best.
✨ XPEach correct answer is worth 10 XP (some levels give more); a missed answer still gives 2 XP for trying.
🔥 StreakFinishing a Daily Practice counts that day. Practicing on back-to-back days grows the streak; skipping a day resets it to 1.

5. Daily Practice

The big card on the home screen serves 10 questions a day, personalized: MathQuest samples across the grade's units and prefers unlocked levels your child hasn't finished or mastered yet — so the plan quietly reviews the weakest spots first. The plan is the same all day for a given grade and refreshes each morning. Finishing it updates the streak and earns coins.

6. The six question types

🔘 Multiple choice

Tap the right answer on a big option card — the most common format in the game (611 levels).

⌨️ Number pad

Type the answer on the on-screen keypad and press Check (443 levels).

✔️✖️ True or false

Read the statement, tap True or False (97 levels).

🃏 Match pairs

Pair up related cards — a digit with its word, a coin with its value (78 levels).

🔢 Order sequence

Tap items in the right order — smallest to largest, first to last (69 levels).

👆 Count & tap

Count the emoji objects, then tap the matching amount (42 levels).

8. Parent Area 👤

Open Profile from the home screen:

  • Grade progress — a row per grade showing stars earned out of the maximum (levels × 3). Tap any row to switch the active grade instantly — progress in other grades is kept.
  • Reset all progress 🧹 — erases the profile, stars, coins, and stickers after a confirmation prompt, then returns to onboarding.

All data is stored locally on the device (nothing is uploaded).

10. Tips & FAQ

Which grade should I pick?

Start with your child's school grade — or one lower if they're new to the topics. Switching later is free and keeps all earned stars, so there's no wrong choice.

My child finished everything!

Mastering all 1,340 levels at 3 stars each is the full quest — plus the sticker collection and a long practice streak.

Can two kids share a device?

MathQuest keeps one profile per device. The Parent Area's reset starts a fresh profile when needed.

Is anything sent online?

No — the profile lives only in the app's local storage on the device.
